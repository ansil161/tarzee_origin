One cloth.
Black.
Worked by hand.

Terzee Origins makes block printed, patchwork and frayed pieces in small runs. Every garment starts black and takes its character from the hand.

Shop the pieces

  1. Cloth

    Heavy cotton twill, poplin and denim, cut boxy and loose with room to move.

  2. Print

    Patterns are block printed by hand, one panel at a time.

  3. Patch

    Squares of cloth are pieced together in four greys, with the seams left showing.

  4. Fray

    Strips of denim are laid by hand and left to fray along the edge.

  5. Stitch

    Black blanket stitch traces the edge by hand, so the work stays visible.

What we hold to

  • Black first.Every piece starts as black cloth. Colour comes from the world around it.
  • Made by hand.The print, the patchwork and the finishing stitch are all done by hand.
  • Small runs.Each drop is made in a limited run, then it moves on.
  • Built to wear.Heavy cloth and honest seams, made to be worn hard.

The cloth we use

Cotton twillHeavy and structured. The base for our jackets.Cotton poplinLight and crisp, cut loose for the heat.Patchwork cottonRaw squares pieced with exposed seams.
