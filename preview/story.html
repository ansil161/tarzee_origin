Our story

Terzee means tailor.

Terzee Origins is a study in one black cloth. Every piece starts the same way — heavy cotton, cut boxy — and leaves the table different: block-printed with lattice and diamond repeats, pieced from raw squares in four greys, or laid with strips of denim and left to fray.

Nothing is hidden. Blanket stitch traces every collar and hem. Patches keep their exposed edges. The hand is always visible, and no two pieces fall the same way.

We make in limited runs and ship across India for free. Drop 01 — AW26 is live now.
