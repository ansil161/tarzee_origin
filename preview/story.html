The name

A word that kept its needle.

  3. 03

What we stand for

Three things, sewn together.

  • 01

    Heritage

    Lattice screens, carved doors, weathered walls. The ornament of old streets, kept patient and precise.

  • 02

    Street

    Auto-rickshaws, cassette radios, the chai stall at midnight. Clothes made to be worn fast and familiar.

  • 03

    Craft

    Blanket stitch, block print, patchwork with exposed edges. The hand is always visible.

The craft

Every piece carries the hand that made it.

Four techniques repeat across Drop 01. Learn to spot them and you can read any Terzee piece from across the street.

Blanket stitch

Collars, hems and appliqué edges traced by hand in black thread.

Block print

Lattice and diamond repeats, pressed in tone on tone. Never two prints alike.

Patchwork

Raw squares in four greys, pieced with the edges left showing.

Frayed appliqué

Strips of black denim laid down and left to fray, one slow line at a time.
