FAQ

Good to know

How does the fit run?
Boxy and true to size. If you sit between sizes or want a roomier drape, size up.
How long does delivery take?
Orders are dispatched in 2–4 days and shipped free across India.
Can I return or exchange?
Yes — returns within 10 days, unworn with tags on.
How do I care for it?
Cold wash inside out, line dry in shade, do not tumble. Frayed and patchwork pieces soften with wear — that is the point.
Why might two pieces look slightly different?
Every piece is finished by hand in limited runs, so small differences are part of the cloth.
